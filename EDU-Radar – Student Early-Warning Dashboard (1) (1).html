How it works

01Data

Attendance, assignments and marks are collected from sample data or your CSV.

02Early warning

Each student gets a 0–100 risk score and a trend before results fall.

03Explain

Every score is split into factors, so you see exactly why.

04Intervene

Smart suggestions: counselling, guidance, tutoring or a parent meeting.

05Track

Mark actions done and compare risk before and after support.
